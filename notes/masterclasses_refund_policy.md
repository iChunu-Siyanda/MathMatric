## Refund Policy for Digital Masterclasses

### 1. General Policy
All masterclass purchases are eligible for a refund within a defined window
after purchase, based on whether the content has been accessed. Once a
masterclass has been downloaded, purchases become final, because MathMatric
cannot verify or restrict further use of downloaded content.

### 2. Eligibility for Refunds
You may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if:
* **Unaccessed Content:** You have not triggered a download of the
  masterclass content. (Streaming a preview, if one is provided, does not
  count as a download and does not forfeit refund eligibility.)
* **Technical Failure:** The content is defective, unplayable, or
  inaccessible due to a system fault on our end, and our support team is
  unable to resolve the issue within 3 business days of notification.
  This applies regardless of download status.
* **Misrepresentation:** The delivered content drastically deviates from
  the advertised syllabus or description. This applies regardless of
  download status.

### 3. Ineligible Refund Scenarios
Refunds will not be granted if:
* You have downloaded the masterclass content, except under the Technical
  Failure or Misrepresentation grounds above.
* More than 7 days have passed since purchase.
* Your account was suspended due to a violation of our Terms of Service
  (e.g., unauthorized redistribution of downloaded content).

### 4. Processing Time
Approved refunds for web or direct card payments will be processed within
5–10 business days. For purchases made via the Apple App Store or Google
Play Store, refunds are handled through their respective app store account portals.
